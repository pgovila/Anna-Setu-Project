The Problem

India wastes Rs. 92,000 crore of food every year -
campuses are a silent contributor.

Institutional kitchens over-produce by 20-40% using fixed static buffers. Food that could nourish people instead becomes methane-emitting landfill or municipal waste - violating both FSSAI regulations and Lucknow Nagar Nigam by-laws. No scalable, legally-grounded digital tool existed to fix this - until now.

How it Works

From Surplus to Shelter in 3 Steps

Anna-Setu runs two parallel tracks - Track A for digital AI compliance and Track B for physical plate-waste hardware.

📋
1

Log the Surplus

Kitchen staff enter what food is left over, when it was cooked, and how it's stored. Takes under 60 seconds.

🤖
2

AI Safety Check

The RAG compliance engine cross-checks FSSAI Regulation 4(2) and LNN Section 14 and returns a 🟢 / 🟡 / 🔴 verdict in real time.

🏠
3

Match & Alert Shelter

The nearest verified Rain Basera shelter is auto-matched by zone and capacity. A WhatsApp-ready alert is generated instantly.

♻️
4

Recycle Plate Waste

Track B: a smart IoT kiosk at the tray-return counter weighs plate scraps, nudges diners, and feeds the on-site bio-recycler.

🌱
5

Fertilizer in 24h

PTC ceramic heating + aerobic aeration converts food scraps into dry, odourless organic fertilizer within 24 hours - zero municipal burden.

📊
6

Predict & Prevent

The demand advisor uses day, weather, and academic calendar signals to calculate optimal cooking quantities - stopping waste before it starts.

Platform Features

Everything in One Place

Available on both Streamlit and Hugging Face - designed for dining hall staff, wardens, and sustainability officers.

⚖️

FSSAI-Grounded Compliance

Every decision cites the exact clause - Regulation 4(2), 4(3), 4(4), 4(5) and Section 7. Legally defensible documentation auto-generated.

🧠

IBM Granite RAG Engine

A retrieval-augmented generation pipeline built on IBM Granite synthesises answers from verified food safety knowledge bases - no hallucinations.

📍

Zone-Based Shelter Matching

6+ verified Lucknow Rain Basera shelters indexed by zone, distance, capacity, and cold-storage availability for instant smart matching.

📱

WhatsApp Alert Generator

One-tap copy of a fully formatted donation alert with food details, pickup location, timing, and official form-B audit reference.

🔮

Smart Demand Forecasting

Predict dining turnout from day-of-week, weather, and exam schedule signals. Optimised cooking targets cut overproduction by up to 20%.

💬

Plain-English Q&A

Ask "Can I donate rice after 3 hours?" and get a clear rule-backed answer drawn from official regulations - accessible to all staff levels.
