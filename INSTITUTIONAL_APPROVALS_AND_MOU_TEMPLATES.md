```text
I am writing to formally request permission to conduct a 14-day operational pilot of "Anna-Setu" in our campus dining hall during December 2026. 

This project is developed as part of the 1M1B (1 Million for 1 Billion) AI for Sustainability Virtual Internship, conducted in collaboration with IBM SkillsBuild and AICTE, under the mentorship of 1M1B leadership.

PROJECT SUMMARY & MOTIVATION:
Institutional dining facilities routinely experience high food waste across two fronts:
1. Kitchen Overproduction: 10% to 30% buffer cooking that results in untouched leftover meals.
2. Diner Plate Waste: Diners taking extra portions onto their plates and discarding leftovers.

Anna-Setu introduces a closed-loop socio-technical framework operating across two synchronized tracks:
- Track A (Software & AI): Uses predictive machine learning to forecast meal attendance, reducing overproduction margins, and evaluates surplus safety against statutory FSSAI (2019) regulations and municipal by-laws using a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) compliance pipeline.
- Track B (Hardware Bio-Recycling Station): A benchtop smart kiosk installed at the tray-return counter that uses a precision plate scale and a behavioral nudge screen to deter over-serving, while channeling plate scraps into an accelerated thermo-mechanical bio-composter that yields nutrient-rich organic bio-fertilizer within 24 hours.

INSTITUTIONAL VALUE FOR THE CAMPUS:
- NAAC & NIRF Sustainability Accreditation: Direct, quantifiable data for SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption) and SDG 13 (Climate Action) institutional reporting.
- Economic Savings: Projected 15% to 25% reduction in kitchen buffer procurement waste.
- Zero Municipal Landfill Burden: Treats 100% of plate scraps on-site into free organic fertilizer for campus landscaping and forestry.
- Positive Social Impact: Wholesome, safety-checked surplus meals safely redirected to empanelled local shelters (Rain Basera Charbagh).

SAFETY, LEGAL COMPLIANCE & ZERO LIABILITY:
1. Good Samaritan Protection: Under Regulation 4(5) and Section 7 of the FSSAI Surplus Food Regulations (2019), donors acting in good faith complying with hygiene guidelines are fully indemnified against civil or criminal liability.
2. Strict Temperature Rules: Only food held within verified safe thermal windows (Hot-held >=65°C or ambient <4 hours) is ever cleared for redistribution. Non-compliant food is diverted strictly to composting.
3. No Disruption to Kitchen Operations: The Track B station is modular, splash-proof (IP65), compact, and operates seamlessly at the dish-clearing counter without slowing dining operations.

PILOT TIMELINE & REQUIREMENTS:
- Setup & Sensor Calibration: December 01 - December 07, 2026.
- 14-Day Live Pilot: December 08 - December 21, 2026.
- Physical Footprint: 2.5 ft x 2 ft counter space at tray clearing point + standard 220V power socket.

We kindly request your formal approval to proceed with setup and calibration. We would welcome the opportunity to demonstrate the working prototype at your convenience.
```

## 📄 DOCUMENT 2: Bilateral Food Recovery & Redistribution Agreement (MoU)

```text
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MoU)
FOR SAFE SURPLUS FOOD RESCUE AND REDISTRIBUTION

This Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) is entered into on this _____ day of November, 2026, by and between:

PARTY A (THE DONOR ESTABLISHMENT):
Anna-Setu Campus Hub, University Dining Services,
1M1B AI for Sustainability Virtual Internship,
Hereinafter referred to as "THE DONOR" (which expression shall include its mess administrators and student leads).

AND

PARTY B (THE VERIFIED DISTRIBUTION PARTNER):
[Rain Basera Charbagh Hub / Lucknow Roti Bank / Empanelled Shelter NGO],
Registration No: ____________________________________
Registered Address: _________________________________
Hereinafter referred to as "THE RECIPIENT" (which expression shall include its coordinators and volunteers).

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
The purpose of this agreement is to formalize a safe, policy-compliant surplus food redistribution network diverting wholesome, unserved institutional cafeteria meals from disposal to food-insecure individuals, in strict compliance with the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) Surplus Food Regulations, 2019.

2. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE DONOR (PARTY A):
2.1 Only unserved, untouched batch surplus prepared in licensed institutional kitchens shall be offered for donation. Under no circumstances will plate waste or post-consumer leftovers be donated.
2.2 The Donor will evaluate every consignment through the Anna-Setu RAG Compliance Engine, verifying that holding times do not exceed statutory ambient thresholds (maximum 3 to 4 hours from cooking).
2.3 The Donor will pack surplus in clean, food-grade, covered containers bearing an Anna-Setu Dispatch Label detailing: Item Name, Dietary Symbol (Veg/Non-Veg), Preparation Time, and Safe Consume-By Deadline.
2.4 The Donor will maintain a daily physical and digital FSSAI Form B Surplus Handover Register.

3. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE RECIPIENT (PARTY B):
3.1 The Recipient agrees to collect surplus within 45 minutes of dispatch notification confirmation.
3.2 The Recipient will transport food in dedicated, clean, covered vehicles shielding food from sunlight, rain, and emissions, adhering to Lucknow Nagar Nigam Transit Protocol Section 14.
3.3 The Recipient guarantees that food received will be distributed directly to needy beneficiaries free of charge and will be consumed prior to the stated "Safe Consume-By" deadline.
3.4 The Recipient will inspect food visually and olfactory upon arrival and countersign the Form B Handover Register.

4. LEGAL INDEMNITY (GOOD SAMARITAN CLAUSE):
In accordance with Regulation 4(5) and Section 7 of the FSSAI (Recovery and Distribution of Surplus Food) Regulations, 2019, the Donor is indemnified against civil or criminal liability resulting from any subsequent handling, third-party transit delay, or storage failure once food is transferred in verified compliant condition at the campus pickup point.

5. TERMINATION:
Either party may terminate this agreement with 7 days written notice.
```
